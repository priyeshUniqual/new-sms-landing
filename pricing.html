Good to Know

Can I join an event for free?

Athletes at covered events can register for free, build a player profile, and access event games and stats for a limited time. Check that your event is covered.

Do I have to use AI to work with my film?

No. The DIY package is for hands-on tagging and stats work, with AI assistance during tagging. The $199.99 service is for AI-assisted stats and highlights.

Can I purchase only highlights or a full game?
