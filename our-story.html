Our Story

Built From The Ground Up.
Built For The Athlete.

Since 2021, we've been building Spark My Sport from the ground up, a patented, AI-powered sports technology platform built around one belief:

Young athletes deserve more than highlights. They deserve a path to grow.

Our founding team brings together business leaders, technologists, coaches, trainers, parents, and former competitive athletes. We've been athletes ourselves, coached the game, raised young athletes, and experienced firsthand the challenges families face trying to help their kids develop and find the right opportunities.

https://anand-profile.vercel.app/

That experience shaped what we're building.

We're not here just to create flashy social media reels or recruiting hype. We're using game film, AI, coaching, data, and community to help student-athletes understand their game, improve their skills, get seen, and reach the next level.

Because success in sports is about more than stats. It's about developing the discipline, confidence, resilience, and character that stays with an athlete long after the final whistle.

Develop The Player. Build The Character. Create The Opportunity.

That's why we built Spark My Sport.
